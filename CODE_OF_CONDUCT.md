# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this to be a project people can contribute to without having to be
thick-skinned about it. Participation should not depend on your experience level,
background, identity, or how confidently you write English.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique the code, the design, or the claim — not the person who wrote it.
- Assume the other person had a reason. Ask what it was before concluding there
  wasn't one.
- Say what you actually think, plainly. Directness and disrespect are different
  things, and the first one is welcome.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change. Scope is a real constraint.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption,
publishing others' private information, or unwelcome attention of any kind.

## Security reports are not conduct reports

Found a vulnerability? See [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) — do not open a public
issue. That is about protecting users, not about behaviour.

## Reporting

Email **conduct@akaion.com**. Reports go to the maintainers, stay confidential,
and are acknowledged within three working days.

We will say what we decided and why. Consequences range from a private word to a
permanent ban, chosen in proportion to what happened and whether it continued
after being raised.

## Scope

This repository, its issues and pull requests, and any space where someone is
representing the project.
