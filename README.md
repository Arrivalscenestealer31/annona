## 🧠 Understanding your new tool

Annona acts as a policy engine for your AI. It makes sure your digital agents work within the bounds you set. Instead of sending your private data to a remote cloud server, Annona processes information locally.

### Data sovereignty
Your data stays on your hard drive. Annona prevents sensitive files from leaving your computer unless you explicitly allow it. This keeps your work private and compliant with data protection standards.

### Task orchestration
You can assign specific tasks to your AI agents. Annona watches these tasks to ensure they finish. If an agent tries to perform an action outside of your established rules, Annona blocks the move.

### Audit logs
Annona records every interaction. You can view these logs at any time to see exactly what your AI agents did and when they did it. This gives you peace of mind and full visibility into your automated processes.

## 🛠️ Using the interface

When you launch Annona, you will see a simple dashboard. 

*   **Status Indicators:** You will see green lights for active agents and red lights for stopped ones.
*   **Settings Menu:** Use this to define your privacy rules. You can toggle specific permissions for different agents.
*   **Activity Feed:** This area shows you a running list of actions. You can filter this list by date or by agent name.

If you need to stop an agent, click the "Stop" button next to its name. The agent will freeze immediately until you decide to restart it.
